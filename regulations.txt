ACADEMIC REGULATIONS

1. Attendance

Students are expected to attend academic activities regularly.

Attendance requirements and eligibility conditions should be checked
from the latest official institutional regulations.

The AI Student Support Assistant should not invent a specific
attendance percentage when it is not available in the knowledge base.

2. Assignments

Students should complete assignments within the deadlines announced
by the department or institution.

Students should follow the required format and submission method.

3. Laboratory Work

Students should attend laboratory sessions regularly.

Laboratory records and practical work should be completed according
to departmental instructions.

4. Academic Discipline

Students are expected to follow academic rules and institutional
instructions.

Students should maintain appropriate academic conduct.

5. Projects

Students should complete project activities according to the
requirements communicated by the department.

Project activities may include:

- Topic selection
- Requirement analysis
- Literature review
- Implementation
- Testing
- Documentation
- Demonstration
- Presentation

6. Internships

Students participating in internships should follow the institution's
requirements for approval, documentation and completion.

7. Deadlines

Students are responsible for completing academic work before the
deadlines announced by the institution or department.

8. Official Information

Rules and regulations can change.

Students should use the latest official institutional document when
checking college-specific regulations.

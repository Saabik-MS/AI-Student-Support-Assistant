STUDENT SUPPORT FAQ

Q: What is Artificial Intelligence?
A: Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science concerned with
building systems that can perform tasks associated with human intelligence.

Q: What is Machine Learning?
A: Machine Learning is a branch of Artificial Intelligence in which systems
learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions.

Q: What is Data Science?
A: Data Science is the process of collecting, processing, analysing and
interpreting data to obtain useful information and insights.

Q: What programming language is useful for AI and Data Science?
A: Python is an important programming language commonly used for
programming, data analysis and machine learning.

Q: What is RAG?
A: Retrieval-Augmented Generation is a technique in which relevant
information is retrieved from a knowledge source before an AI model
generates an answer.

Q: What is a project?
A: A project is a practical activity in which students identify a problem,
design a solution, implement it, test it and document the results.

Q: How can students get project guidance?
A: Students can contact the appropriate department faculty for academic
project guidance and support.

Q: Where can students find examination information?
A: Students should refer to the latest official institutional
announcements and examination information.

Q: Where can students find syllabus information?
A: Students should refer to the approved syllabus and official
department or institutional academic documents.

Q: How can students improve their technical skills?
A: Students can improve technical skills through regular programming
practice, laboratory work, projects, assignments and self-learning.

Q: What skills are useful for AI and Data Science students?
A: Useful skills include Python, SQL, statistics, data analysis,
machine learning, artificial intelligence, problem solving and project
development.

Q: What should students do when they have an academic doubt?
A: Students can refer to available academic resources and contact
appropriate faculty for academic guidance.

Q: What should students do before an examination?
A: Students should check the latest examination instructions, schedule,
hall ticket information and required materials.

ACADEMIC SCHEDULE INFORMATION

1. Academic Schedule

An academic schedule contains information about important academic
activities during a semester.

It may include:

- Semester beginning
- Semester ending
- Working days
- Class activities
- Internal assessments
- Assignment periods
- Laboratory activities
- Project reviews
- Semester examinations
- Holidays

2. Checking the Schedule

Students should regularly check official institutional or department
announcements for the latest academic schedule.

3. Changes

Academic schedules may change because of institutional decisions,
examination arrangements or other academic requirements.

Students should follow the latest officially communicated schedule.

4. Assignment Schedule

Assignment submission dates should be followed according to the
announcements made by the department or institution.

5. Examination Schedule

Students should check official examination announcements for
examination dates, subjects, venues and related instructions.

6. Project Schedule

Project reviews, presentations and submission dates should be followed
according to the schedule communicated by the department.

7. Important Note

The AI Student Support Assistant should not invent specific academic
dates when the current official schedule is not available in the
knowledge base.

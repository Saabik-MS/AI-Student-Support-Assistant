AI STUDENT SUPPORT ASSISTANT - STUDENT INFORMATION

1. Department Overview

The Artificial Intelligence and Data Science department focuses on
Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Programming,
Statistics, Data Analytics and related technologies.

The department helps students develop technical knowledge, problem-solving
skills and practical abilities required for academic projects and future
careers.

2. Important Academic Activities

Students may participate in several academic activities, including:

- Theory classes
- Laboratory sessions
- Assignments
- Internal assessments
- Semester examinations
- Mini projects
- Major projects
- Internships
- Technical seminars
- Presentations

Students are expected to complete academic activities within the
deadlines communicated by the institution or department.

3. Learning Skills

Students in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science can develop skills in:

- Python programming
- Machine Learning
- Artificial Intelligence
- Data Science
- Data Analytics
- SQL
- Database Management
- Statistics
- Basic programming concepts
- Problem solving
- Data visualization

Practical knowledge can be improved through programming exercises,
laboratory work, projects and regular practice.

4. Academic Guidance

Students can approach their department faculty for academic guidance,
subject-related doubts, project support and general academic assistance.

Students should refer to official institutional announcements for
important academic information and deadlines.

5. Project Support

Students may require guidance while selecting project topics, preparing
datasets, developing applications, testing systems and preparing project
documentation.

For project-related academic guidance, students should consult the
appropriate faculty or department.

6. Internship Guidance

Internships can help students gain practical exposure to technologies,
tools and workplace practices.

Students should carefully check internship requirements, duration,
documentation and approval procedures according to institutional
instructions.

7. Student Responsibilities

Students should:

- Attend classes regularly.
- Complete assignments on time.
- Maintain laboratory records where required.
- Participate in academic activities.
- Follow examination instructions.
- Complete project work within announced deadlines.
- Check official announcements regularly.

8. Academic Records

Students should maintain their academic information, including subject
details, examination information, assignment status, project progress
and other relevant academic records.

9. Career Preparation

Students interested in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science careers
can improve their employability by developing technical skills,
communication skills, problem-solving abilities and project experience.

Useful areas for career preparation include Python, SQL, Machine Learning,
Data Analytics, Artificial Intelligence, Git, project development and
technical interview preparation.

10. General Student Support

The AI Student Support Assistant can help students understand academic
concepts, find information from the available knowledge base, perform
simple calculations and remember selected personal study information.

For official college-specific rules, dates and announcements, students
should refer to the latest official institutional documents.
